# ⚖️ Terms of Service & Delivery Protocol
**Chef Apedo Foods** | Accra, Ghana  

By placing an order through the Chef Apedo Foods platform, you agree to the following operational parameters.

## 📦 1. Order Fulfillment & Delivery
* **Delivery Zones:** We currently deliver exclusively to the University of Ghana campus (Main, Evandy, Pentagon) and surrounding East Legon areas. Orders placed outside these GPS zones will be canceled.
* **Courier Wait Times:** Dispatch riders will attempt to contact the provided phone number upon arrival. If the customer is unreachable after **10 minutes**, the rider is instructed to return the meal to the kitchen to maintain food safety standards. No refunds are issued for missed hand-offs.

## 💳 2. Refund & Cancellation Policy
Because our products are highly perishable, prepared-to-order meals, our return windows are strict.
* **Pre-Preparation:** You may cancel an order for a full refund *only* if the order status is still "Order Received."
* **In-Progress:** Once the kitchen advances the order to "Preparing," cancellations and refunds are no longer permitted.
* **Quality Control:** If an order arrives incorrect or missing items, you must contact our support line within **30 minutes** of the "Delivered" timestamp for a partial refund or replacement. 

## 🛡️ 3. Fraud Prevention
We reserve the right to ban phone numbers or user accounts associated with fake orders, abusive language toward our delivery team, or fraudulent chargebacks.
