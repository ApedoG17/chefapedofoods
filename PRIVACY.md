# 🔒 Privacy & Cookie Policy
**Chef Apedo Foods** | Accra, Ghana  
*Last Updated: September 2026*

This document outlines how Chef Apedo Foods collects, processes, and protects user data across our campus delivery platform. We strictly adhere to data minimization principles: we only collect what is necessary to deliver your food.

## 📊 1. Data Collection Map

| Data Point | Purpose | Retention & Storage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First & Last Name** | Order identification | Stored securely in Supabase |
| **Phone Number** | Dispatch routing & SMS updates | Stored securely in Supabase |
| **GPS / Hostel Location** | Delivery fulfillment | Stored securely in Supabase |
| **Payment Details** | MoMo/Card Processing | **Never Stored.** Handled by Hubtel |

## 🤝 2. Third-Party Integrations
We route specific data through verified, encrypted third-party vendors to operate the platform:
* **Supabase:** Provides AES-256 encrypted database storage and authentication.
* **Hubtel:** Processes all financial transactions. We do not touch or store Mobile Money PINs or credit card numbers.
* **Agoo SMS:** Receives phone numbers temporarily to dispatch order status texts and opt-in promotional broadcasts.

## 🍪 3. Cookie Policy
We use **Strictly Necessary Cookies** only. These are required for the website to function securely:
* **Session Cookies:** Keeps you logged in and maintains your active cart.
* **Authentication Tokens:** Verifies secure requests between the frontend and Supabase.
> *Note: Because we do not use third-party analytics trackers (like Meta Pixel or Google Analytics advertising tags) to track you across other sites, an intrusive cookie consent banner is not legally required for this platform.*
